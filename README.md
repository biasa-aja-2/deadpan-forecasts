A deadpan weather forecast for Velmbrook, a city that does not exist — light soup drizzle at 62 degrees, clearing by noon, with the clouds apologising twice and promising to be rain next time.

## The forecast

> Forecast for Velmbrook: light drizzle of warm soup at 62 degrees, clearing by noon; the clouds have apologised twice and promise to be rain next time.

150 characters, one line, delivered in the flat tone of a TV meteorologist reading off the map.

## Why this line

One absurd premise (soup falls from the sky instead of rain), one deadpan twist (the clouds behave like a flaky contractor, apologising twice and promising to do better next time), and concrete numbers that make the invented place feel real: 62 degrees, clearing by noon. No pile of random absurdity — just a straight-faced forecast for a city that never existed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the forecast to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), pure Python standard library, and verifies the gate itself first: 1–160 characters, a single line, real text.
